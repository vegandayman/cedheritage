What is EDHeritage?

← Back to Checker

EDHeritage is a Commander (EDH) sub-format that restricts the card pool to Standard-legal premier sets. It removes cards designed specifically for Commander, supplemental power creep, and crossover IP, forcing players to build decks using cards designed primarily for traditional 1v1 Magic.

Core Rules of EDHeritage

  1. Base Rules: Standard EDH deck construction, singleton requirements, and color identity rules apply. The official EDH banlist is fully enforced.
  2. Premier Set Requirement: A card is legal only if it has at least one printing in a Standard-legal premier set.
  3. No Supplemental Exclusives: Cards printed exclusively in supplemental products (such as Commander decks, Modern Horizons, or straight-to-Legacy sets) are banned.
  4. The "Your Commander" Ban: Any card containing the exact phrase "your commander" in its Oracle text is illegal. This removes the three cards printed into standard with the Throne of Edlraine Brawl decks.
  5. No Universes Beyond: All "Universes Beyond" cards (such as Doctor Who, Fallout, or Lord of the Rings) are banned, regardless of how or where they were printed. The exception is for in-universe reskins, such as Through the Omenpaths.
  6. The Commander vs 99: The EDHeritage rules apply to the 99, not the commander. As WotC insist on printing the best commanders directly into eternal formats, and not via standard, the heritage legandary card pool is currently 1,462 cards, compared to 3,363 in EDH. Purists of the format will run a 100% Heritage deck, and that's fine too!
